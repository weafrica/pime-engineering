About Us

Pime Engineering is a precision mechanical engineering company based in Benoni, Gauteng. We specialise in precision machining, metal fabrication and welding, mechanical installation, plant maintenance, and equipment repairs for clients across a range of industrial sectors.

Guided by our motto — precision, quality, reliability — we combine hands-on workshop expertise with careful attention to tolerance, finish, and turnaround time, delivering components and services that keep our clients' plants and equipment running.

Our Mission

To deliver precise, high-quality mechanical engineering solutions — from custom-machined components to plant maintenance and equipment repairs — that our clients can rely on, every time.
